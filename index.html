The Oasis

A high-energy lounge
with a fine-dining soul.

Dune is a premium, top-rated pub and lounge on the 5th floor of Apurupa Towers, Jubilee Hills. Sandstone arches, hieroglyph-etched walls and firelight give way to an open rooftop where Hyderabad's skyline glows past a golden owl guardian.

By day it's relaxed and sun-soaked. By night — Friday through Sunday especially — it turns into the city's most infectious weekend floor, with a top-tier sound system, chefs who greet your table personally, and a bar pouring custom cocktails, hard liquor, craft beer and wine.
